Organise a dump into captures

Splits a folder of loose images into captures by time and distance, links captures of the same structure from different missions, and creates a folder for each one.

Source
Capture rules
A capture is a run of consecutive shots with no gap longer than the time above, staying within the distance of where it started, and (when camera pitch is available) beginning at a nadir shot. Captures whose centres are within the structure distance are the same structure; repeat visits are shown together so you can decide whether they were one capture or several.
